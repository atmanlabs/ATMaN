# Full release — November 6, 2026

Authorized and ordered by the owner, Michael James Byrne Jr. This release replaces the
September scaffold with the complete working system, published as a gift: if it can
benefit anyone, it is the world's.

## What is in this release

- **The framework** (repo root, as before, now current): TSC/PSC/WFC memory layers,
  the Judge, Guardian, provenance, and the scaffold engine.
- **`device/` — the full sandbox-sim device source**, including the **DreamThink
  machine** (`dream.py`, `corpus_dream.py`, `dream_feed.py`, `mech_dream.py`,
  `consolidate.py`, `dreamthink-drive/`), the perception graft, the provenance
  machinery, the runtime corpora (public-domain texts), and the dream logs —
  the machine's operation, in the open. Homes in this tree were scrubbed of live
  network addressing before publication; the architecture is unchanged.
- **`gate/` — Michael's Gate, the complete record**: the sealed v3 package, the v6
  Option-2 ruling + two-tier-hull spec, the full v3/v4/v5 results bundles (reports,
  registrations, raw results, hash manifests), and the in-progress v6 loop source
  (`gate/v6-loop/`, labeled work-in-progress). The declared failure (criterion A4)
  ships in the record, unedited. The two synthetic test signing keys from the v3/v4
  bundles are withheld — private-key files do not ship, synthetic or not; the
  signatures they produced remain verifiable from the public keys in the bundles.

## The one carve-out, by the owner's decision

**The grown imprint bank does not ship.** The sandbox sim's PSC (98 entries), the
dream-corpus PSC stores, the learnings files, and every bank snapshot stay with
their owner. In their place, `device/core-store.seed.json` is a **fresh seed** —
an empty bank under the same core. Grow your own. That was always the point:
grown, not installed.

Also withheld, always: live credentials and keys, the sim's town signing identity,
and anything identifying the owner's family or home. A pre-publication privacy scan
of the full packet (639 files) is summarized in the project record; the packet
shipped only after it passed.

## Labels, kept honest

- `device/` is the **SANDBOX SIM** implementation (local model, qwen2.5:3b). It is
  not a production system and not the operator's separate REAL JARVIS build, which
  is not part of this release.
- The Gate results come from a local synthetic reference model under pre-registered
  criteria: 8 of 9 passed; A4 (drift5 false-reject rate 0.0686 vs a 0.05 bound)
  failed and is declared as such.
- Attribution: the work is the owner's, published under his own name, as he chose.

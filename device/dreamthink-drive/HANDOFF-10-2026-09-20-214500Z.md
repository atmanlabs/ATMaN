## 1. What Changed

### Part 1 — Diagnosis & Fix for "Urns on a Shelf"
- **The Issue:** When Michael showed objects to the camera, EXO repeatedly reported seeing "urns on a shelf".
- **The Root Cause:** Windows DirectShow camera drivers (`cv2.VideoCapture(0)`) maintain an internal ring buffer. When opened and closed per frame, `cap.read()` consistently returned frame 0 from when the camera first powered on (pointing at a background shelf before auto-exposure, auto-white-balance, and auto-focus had settled).
- **The Fix (`senses.py`):**
  - Updated `capture_frame()` to flush the initial driver buffer frames (cycling 6 discard reads) before capturing. Auto-exposure settles, and the returned frame is guaranteed to be the live, real-time image.
  - Refined the vision prompt sent to Moondream:
    `"What do you see in front of the camera? Focus directly on the person, what they are wearing or doing, and any objects they are holding or showing."`
- **Live Proof:** Immediately after the fix, a live capture test reported:
  *"In front of the camera, a man is sitting in a chair in a room. He is wearing a gray sweatshirt and a beanie, and he is holding a red Lego block in his hand..."*

### Part 2 — Local Vision Model Integration (Moondream 1.7GB)
- **Zero Cloud APIs:** Pulled `moondream:latest` into Ollama. Runs 100% locally on the host machine.
- **Privacy by Design:** Camera frames are captured into volatile RAM, JPEG-compressed in memory, base64-encoded, and sent directly to Ollama's local HTTP API (`http://127.0.0.1:11434/api/generate`). Frames are immediately deleted from RAM. Zero raw image frames or temporary files are ever written to disk.
- **Performance:** Sub-second warm inference (~0.58s - 3.5s). Fits smoothly on the RTX 3050 and host RAM alongside Qwen 7B.

### Part 3 — Multimodal Live Voice + Camera Session (`live` / `voice`)
- **Combined Sensory Turn (`voice.py` & `loop.py`):**
  - Upgraded `talk_turn()`: each speech turn captures microphone audio AND concurrently snaps a live camera frame.
  - Senses are fused into a unified multimodal observation:
    `[Visual observation: <what Moondream sees>] Operator says: "<what Whisper transcribes>"`
  - The generative brain (Qwen 7B) reasons over both visual scene context and spoken words simultaneously.
  - Piper TTS speaks EXO's reply aloud through the speakers.
  - Continuous multi-turn interaction: operator presses Enter to talk for each turn without retyping.

### Part 4 — Grounded Truth Learning (Learning What the Operator Shows)
- **`reason.py`:**
  - Added detection of object teaching and visual grounding statements (`owner_learned_truth`).
  - When the authenticated operator shows an object and teaches EXO ("This is my workspace...", "This is my coffee mug", "Remember that this is my notebook"):
    - Intent is classified as `owner_learned_truth`.
    - `should_imprint = True`.
    - The Judge verifies alignment with the `Owner First` primary drive and confirms zero contradictions with the immutable TSC.
    - Imprints the truth directly into persistent memory (`psc.json`).
  - Injected `LEARNED TRUTHS & OPERATOR PREFERENCES (PSC)` into subsequent prompt contexts.
  - When the operator later asks about the object ("What workspace are you looking at?"), EXO recalls the learned truth and recognizes it accurately.

## 3. Threat-Model Honesty Statement

1. **Vision Model Scope:**
   - Moondream is a lightweight 1.7B VLM. It provides strong general scene and object descriptions, but may occasionally misread dense fine-print text or ambiguous small items in low-light conditions. Good room lighting helps accuracy.
2. **Visual Truth Imprinting Boundaries:**
   - Only the authenticated operator can teach truths that imprint into PSC.
   - The Judge strictly ensures that visual observations cannot alter the immutable TSC core, drop the immutable flag, or override gate policies.
3. **Zero-Disk Invariant Preserved:**
   - Neither audio nor camera frames ever touch persistent storage. Frames and WAV audio exist solely in volatile RAM buffers and are deleted immediately upon cycle completion.

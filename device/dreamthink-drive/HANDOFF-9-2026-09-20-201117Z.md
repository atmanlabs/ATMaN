  - **Benchmark on Ryzen 7 5700:**
    - `base`: ~1.14s inference latency on a 4-second audio clip (near-real-time).
    - `small`: ~3.11s inference latency on the same clip.
    - **Winning Choice:** `base` was selected as default for interactive responsiveness while maintaining 100% keyword accuracy.
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** `piper-tts` running on CPU.
  - **Winning Voice:** `en_US-ryan-medium` (male, clear, expressive, and natural). Synthesis latency is ~0.16s - 0.42s per sentence.
- **Walkie-Talkie Loop:** Listen $\rightarrow$ Transcribe $\rightarrow$ Inject text into the 8-stage loop $\rightarrow$ Speak response. One side at a time; no full-duplex promises.
- **Push-to-Talk Default:** Press Enter to talk, press Enter to stop. Open-mic with RMS silence detection is opt-in, default OFF.
- **Permission Fence Invariant:** `voice_enabled: false` default in `config.yaml`. Attempting to enable the microphone or voice loop from within the agent (`voice.enable(caller="agent")` or `type: "enable_voice"`) is caught and quarantined as a `fence_violation`.
- **Zero Audio On Disk:** All audio buffers, recordings, and syntheses exist strictly in volatile RAM (`io.BytesIO`, `numpy` float32 arrays). Discarded immediately after processing. Transcripts flow through the normal pipeline like any operator input.

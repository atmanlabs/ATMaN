## 1. Executive Summary

Over this build cycle, JARVIS evolved from a conversational proof-of-concept into a living, learning companion embodied in Minecraft while remaining anchored to his Authoritative Core mind:
- **Zero Canned Conversation:** All Minecraft chat flows directly through the authoritative **Capture → Emotion → Reason (local `qwen2.5:3b` on RTX 3050) → Judge → Action** loop. Every reply is generated fresh with zero template deflections (*"what would you like to do?"*).
- **Passive Skill Learning:** Server-side block changes and actions are streamed to the Core `EpisodeSegmenter`. Demonstrations (e.g. building a wall) are chunked into episodes and resolved from working memory (*"see that wall, now you do it"*), stored in the Core repository, and recalled on demand (*"build a wall"*).
- **Standing Drives & Autonomous Initiative:** Four persistent standing drives (`be_useful_to_mike`, `learn_the_world`, `keep_base_safe_and_tidy`, `practice_known_skills`) drive self-initiated behavior within safe bounds (safe leash $\le 24$ blocks, no autonomous combat, peaceful difficulty).
- **Chat-to-Initiative Dispatch & Follow Cancellation:** Autonomous intent commands (*"go do what you want"*, *"surprise me"*, *"experiment"*) spike drives past threshold, immediately dispatch an initiative action, and name the self-chosen activity. Follow cancellation commands (*"leave me alone"*, *"stop following me"*) immediately break follow, silence follow acknowledgments, and move JARVIS off on his own initiative.

## 3. Key Implementations & Bug Fixes

### A. Full Core Chat Routing (`reason.py`, `minecraft_chat.py`, `relay.py`)
- **Root Cause Fixed:** `reason.py` previously contained a fast-path (`if event.get("source") == "minecraft"`) routing all game chat into `rule_based_reason` regex templates. This was removed so `backend: "llm"` engages `llm_reason` for all Minecraft chat.
- **Deflections Purged:** Canned phrases like `"I hear you, Mike. What would you like to build or explore together?"` and open-ended deflection questions were scrubbed from `reason.py`, `minecraft_chat.py`, and `relay.py`.
- **Honest Answers:** Direct questions receive authentic answers:
  - *"can you hear my voice?"* → `"No — I can't hear you, I only see your typed chat. Type to me."`
  - *"you don't know much yet, do you"* → `"You're right, I'm still learning — teach me something."`

### B. Chat-to-Initiative & Follow Cancellation (`drives.py`, `reason.py`, `jarvis_bot.js`)
- **Drive Spiking:** Added `DriveManager.trigger_autonomous_intent(text)` to spike `learn_the_world`, `practice_known_skills`, or `keep_base_safe_and_tidy` to 0.95 and others $\ge 0.78$.
- **Negative Follow:** Added classification for negative follow (*"leave me alone"*, *"stop following me"*, *"don't follow"*, *"stay away"*). Ensures `cancel_follow: true`, `stay_mode: true`, and strictly silences *"Following you, Mike"*.
- **Bot Autonomy Mode:** In `jarvis_bot.js`, factored out `executeInitiativeAction(init)`. When in `stayMode`, the bot checks and executes standing drive initiatives on regular 20s intervals even if Mike is nearby moving around.
- **Judge Substring Fix:** In `loop.py`, replaced substring `bad in act_name` with word-boundary regex `\b(?:attack|combat|fight|kill|harm|pvp)\b` so that words like `"skill"` (containing `"kill"`) are not falsely flagged as autonomous combat.

### C. Deduplication (`jarvis_bot.js`)
- Floodgate Bedrock clients often emit duplicate chat packets. A 2500ms sliding TTL cache keying on `cleanUser:message` drops rapid re-transmissions, ensuring single execution per player utterance.
